The MATSim Association has published a new survey for existing and new MATSim users.

The survey is intended to help the association better understand who is using MATSim, how MATSim is being used, and where future support and development efforts can have the most impact.

Please take a few minutes to fill out the [MATSim user survey](https://tally.so/r/1AJWaQ).

We would also appreciate it if you shared the survey with colleagues and collaborators who are using, evaluating, or planning to use MATSim.
