The MATSim User Meeting 2026 will take place in Paris on **Monday, September 28, 2026**, together with hEART 2026.

Abstract submission is still open through EasyChair, and registration is now open as well.

Please visit the [MUM2026 page](/conferences/mum2026/) to submit your abstract and the [MUM2026 registration page](/conferences/mum2026/register/) to check your membership status and register.

Current MATSim Association members attend free of charge as part of their membership benefits. Non-members are also very welcome.
