The **4th International Symposium on Multi-Agent Freight Transport Simulation (FreighTSim)** will take place in Berlin, Germany on **Wednesday, September 9, 2026**.
This symposium is also the **4th Meeting of MATSim Users focusing on freight transport**.

Abstract submission and registration are now open via [e-mail](mailto:freightsim@dlr.de).

Please visit the [FreighTSim 2026 page](/conferences/freightsim2026/) for more information.

The event is free of charge.
