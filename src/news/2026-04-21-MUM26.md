MATSim Association is happy to announce that the MATSim User Meeting 2026 will be held in conjunction with the
14th Symposium of the European Association for Research in Transportation (hEART 2026) in Paris, France.

The user meeting will take place on **Monday, September 28, 2026**, the day before the hEART conference begins.

The Annual General Meeting of the MATSim Association will be held in conjunction with the user meeting.

The call for papers is now open. We are accepting submissions of 2-3 page extended abstracts describing your recent MATSim work.

Please visit the [MUM 2026 page](/conferences/mum2026) to submit your abstract.
